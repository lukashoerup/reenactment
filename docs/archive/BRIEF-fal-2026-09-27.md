# Brief: re-enactment test for Danske Drabssager — generated versions (A, B)

For a Claude Code cloud session with `FAL_KEY` set in its environment. Lukas is on
his phone; he wants the result, not questions.

## What exists
- `audio/episode.mp3` is not in the repo (copyright, 43 MB). Fetch it from the public
  feed: `python3 fetch_audio.py` (reads https://rss.podplaystudio.com/1103.xml, episode
  "Det Brændende Lig"). The feed can insert ads, which shifts the clock: the cut assumes
  "Bålets flammer lokker…" starts at 97.7 s. Check that first (faster-whisper on
  95–100 s); if it has moved, shift every time in `gen/shots.json` and `assemble.py`.
- Version C (Blender 3D + real fire footage) is already made: `out/reenact_C.mp4`.
  Same scene, same cut, same sound. Your job is A and B so the three can be compared.
- `gen/shots.json` — 8 shots of the cold open (01:34.50–01:59.35), timed to the
  narration, with look, cast and prompts for style A (photographic re-enactment) and
  B (charcoal and ink).
- `gen/generate.py` — fal.ai pipeline, resumable, with a spend cap.
- `post.py`, `assemble.py` — house film look and the edit (narration + rain + fire).

## Rules
1. **Preflight first.** `python3 gen/generate.py preflight`. If `FAL_KEY` is missing,
   write that to `QUESTIONS.md` and stop — do not look for keys anywhere else.
2. **Budget.** `MAX_USD=30` per style. The pipeline refuses past it; do not raise it.
3. **Never show a face.** People from behind or as silhouettes. No body, no injuries.
   Nothing that can pass for an archive photo. No text in frame.
4. **Park questions, do not wait.** Anything you need Lukas for goes in `QUESTIONS.md`;
   carry on with what does not depend on it.

## Steps (per style, A first)
1. `gen/generate.py keys A` → makes 4 candidates for S1 only and stops.
2. Look at `gen/A/sheets/S1.png` (use the Read tool on the image). Pick with
   `gen/generate.py pick A S1 <i>`. S1 sets the look for all other shots.
3. `gen/generate.py keys A` again → the other 7 shots, made from the S1 frame.
4. Review every sheet. Reject a candidate if: a face or eyes show; hands or limbs are
   wrong; there is text; it looks like CGI, plastic or a video game; clothes differ from
   S1; the era is wrong (1999). Prefer imperfect, documentary framing and strong
   silhouettes. If all 4 fail, delete that shot's `keys` in `state.json` and regenerate
   once with a sharper prompt; note what you changed.
5. `gen/generate.py clips A` → 2 takes of 5 s per shot. Review each take by extracting
   frames at 0.5 s, 2 s and 4 s (`ffmpeg -ss … -frames:v 1`). Reject morphing, melting,
   sudden changes of clothes, and anything that moves too fast. Pick with `take`.
6. `gen/generate.py frames A`, then `python3 assemble.py out/reenact_A.mp4`.
7. Watch the result: extract one frame per shot plus the three cuts on
   "Et dyr? / Affald? / Eller et menneske?" and check picture against narration.
8. Repeat for B.

## Deliver
- `out/reenact_A.mp4`, `out/reenact_B.mp4`, the contact sheets, and `out/NOTES.md`:
  what was rejected and why, spend per style, and the two or three shots that are
  weakest. Publish one page with all three versions side by side (A, B, C).
